Burn It For Me

A mixtape feature for Spotify. Pick the songs, decorate the disc, record a hello, then hand it to a friend.

Burn a mixtape Open one a friend sent
  1. 1

    Pick the songs

    Fill Side A and Side B, just like a real tape.

  2. 2

    Decorate the disc

    Stickers, gel pens, photos and a handwritten label.

  3. 3

    Say hi before track 1

    Record a hello or write it on the liner notes.

  4. 4

    Burn and send

    Your friend unwraps it and keeps it on their shelf.

Best on your phone. On iPhone, tap Share then Add to Home Screen to open it full screen.

Where it started

The moodboard

From my Project 2 direction, Pass the Aux. Hand-drawn CDs, scribbled labels and loud color only in the sharing moment grew into Burn It For Me.
